Cybersecurity GRC Assessment & Risk Management: Namaa Digital Solutions

Independent practical learning project · Nov 2026 to present

A simulated assessment of a hypothetical Saudi digital services company of about 100 employees, covering governance, IAM, privileged access, logging, vulnerability management, third-party security and data protection.

  • Framework: NCA ECC 2:2024, with PDPL and NCNICC 1:2025 where relevant.
  • Method: Map assets, threats, vulnerabilities and controls. Score risk by likelihood and impact, inherent vs. residual, then prioritize remediation.
  • Findings: Excessive privileges, inactive privileged accounts, weak access reviews and privileged session monitoring.
  • Recommended: Least privilege, access reviews, PAM, MFA, better logging, stronger access revocation.
  • Deliverables: Gap analysis, risk register and matrix, evidence, treatment plans, policies, third-party risk assessment, and a GRC dashboard.

Tools: Microsoft Office Suite, Power BI
